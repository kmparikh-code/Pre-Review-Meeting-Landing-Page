Documents You May Need for Your Meeting

Depending on what you'd like to discuss, you may need some of these documents. Review this list while taking the survey or after you complete it.

For Investment Planning Discussion:

  • ☐ Recent statements for investment accounts
  • ☐ Any recent trades or portfolio changes

For Lifetime Income Planning Discussion:

  • ☐ Social Security statements
  • ☐ Pension benefit statements
  • ☐ Annuity contracts

For Tax Planning Discussion:

  • ☐ Most recent tax return (2024 if filed)
  • ☐ Estimated tax payments made this year
  • ☐ Any tax notices or correspondence from IRS/state

For Estate & Legacy Planning Discussion:

  • ☐ Current will or trust documents
  • ☐ Beneficiary designations (retirement accounts, life insurance)
  • ☐ Power of attorney and healthcare directive

For Cash Flow Planning Discussion:

  • ☐ Recent bank statements
  • ☐ Budget or spending records (if you track them)

For Real Estate Planning Discussion:

  • ☐ Property appraisals (if available)
  • ☐ Mortgage statements
  • ☐ Rental income/expense records (if applicable)

For Protection & Insurance Planning Discussion:

  • ☐ Current insurance policies (life, disability, long-term care)
  • ☐ Recent insurance statements or declarations pages

For Charitable Planning Discussion:

  • ☐ List of charitable organizations you support
  • ☐ Donor-advised fund statements (if applicable)

For Generational Planning Discussion:

  • ☐ Family tree or list of family members involved in planning
  • ☐ Existing family wealth transfer plans or trust documents
  • ☐ Education funding accounts (529 plans, UTMA/UGMA accounts)
  • ☐ Any family governance documents or family mission statements

For Banking & Credit Planning Discussion:

  • ☐ Current bank account statements (checking, savings, money market)
  • ☐ Credit card statements and balances
  • ☐ Outstanding loan information (personal loans, lines of credit)
  • ☐ Credit reports (if available)

For Private Business Planning Discussion:

  • ☐ Business financial statements
  • ☐ Buy-sell agreements
  • ☐ Business valuation (if available)

For Outside Account Review:

  • ☐ Recent statements for accounts we don't currently manage
  • ☐ Account numbers and institution names
  • ☐ Login credentials (if you'd like us to view them together)

We look forward to seeing you soon!
